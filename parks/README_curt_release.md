# parks_v1_curt / parks_v1_curt_x4 — parks_v1 with curtailment

Generated by `scripts/parks/run_parks_v1.py curt_apply|curt_tables --chain configs/round2/PARKS_v1_curt(_x4).yaml`
(generator repo, branch `curtailment`); code in `curtailment/` and `parks/curtail.py`; report
`~/Work/FL_Contribution/reports/curtailment_synthesis_v1.md`; specification
`~/Work/FL_Contribution/reports/curtailment_modelling_review.md` §6.

- Input: the unchanged `parks_v1` release (same 90 parks, same period 2023-07-24 00:00 → 2026-08-31 23:00 UTC,
  27,240 h). All `parks_v1` columns are kept **unchanged except `power_park`**.
- `parks_v1_curt`: realistic grid curtailment (area targets × 1). `parks_v1_curt_x4`: grid curtailment
  amplified to 4 × the area targets (≈ SH level 2015–17); market and environment layers identical.
  Every grid event of `parks_v1_curt` is also in `parks_v1_curt_x4` (common random numbers).

## Layers (settlement order, per quarter-hour)

```
P_avail = power_park of parks_v1                       (free x wake, aging included)
P_env   = P_avail * sum_g share_g (1 - m_env,g)        bat permit masks per turbine group
P_mkt   = P_avail * sum_g share_g (1 - m_env,g)(1 - m_mkt,g)   negative-price shut-down per group
P_obs   = min(P_mkt, s * P_inst)                       redispatch setpoint s in {0, 0.3, 0.6, 1}
share_g = power_t<g> / power_park_free,  P_inst = parks.csv:capacity_kw x 1000 W
```

Hourly values are the means of the four quarter-hours (hourly inputs are constant within the hour).
`availability` stays the placeholder 1.0 (outages are a later layer, before the environment layer).

## New and changed columns of `synth_<park_id>.parquet`

| column | unit | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `power_park` | W | **actual (curtailed) power** = hourly mean of P_obs — the target |
| `power_park_avail` | W | available power = `power_park` of parks_v1 |
| `env_factor` | – | hourly mean of Σ_g share_g (1 − m_env,g) (bat permits only) |
| `mkt_factor` | – | hourly mean of Σ_g share_g (1 − m_mkt,g) (negative prices only) |
| `grid_setpoint` | – | hourly mean of the redispatch setpoint s (1 = free) |
| `loss_env`, `loss_mkt`, `loss_grid` | W | loss per layer; sum = `power_park_avail − power_park` |
| `curt_flag` | 0/1 | 1 if any layer reduces the power in the hour (total loss > 0) |
| `grid_event_id` | str | id `<node>:<start UTC yyyymmddTHHMM>` of the binding grid event (empty otherwise) |
| `neg_block_len_h` | h | length of the negative DA price block the hour belongs to (0 otherwise) |

A setpoint below the current power does not reduce anything; `grid_setpoint < 1` therefore marks the
operator-side instruction, `curt_flag` the realised reduction.

## Reconstructing single-layer scenarios

- only environment: `power_park_avail × env_factor` (exact)
- only market: `power_park_avail × mkt_factor` (exact)
- only grid: `min(power_park_avail, grid_setpoint × P_inst)` — exact when the setpoint is constant within
  the hour, otherwise an upper bound (min is concave in s)
- available power for "train on P_avail" experiments: `power_park_avail`
- all layers but one: subtract the respective `loss_*` from the total loss (`loss_grid` depends on the earlier
  layers, because the cap acts on P_mkt)

## Model in brief

- **Grid** (report 6.2): parks within 10 km form a node (single linkage) and share all events. Per node:
  never affected with p0 (A1 0.1, A2–A4 0.2, A5 0.5, A6 1.0 = no grid layer), participation B_n ~ Beta;
  start rate λ = c_{g,y} B_n exp(a_g + b_g CF_DA) u_g(d) with the national day-ahead onshore CF and a daily
  AR(1) disturbance shared within an area (correlated across areas); lognormal duration (capped at 168 h),
  first setpoint 0/30/60 % (78/12/10 %), after a 0 % event optionally a short 30/60 % release segment.
  c_{g,y} is calibrated per area and year on a virtual fleet to the area targets (see the calibration file).
- **Market** (report 6.3): cohort per turbine group (commissioning date, unit rating) with strike and §51
  rule; post-EEG (p* = 0) from 1 Jan of commissioning year + 21. A park reacts to a negative block with a
  probability that rises with the margin below its threshold (θ fitted to Hirth 2026).
- **Environment** (report 6.4): bat permits for groups commissioned ≥ 2010 (60 % standard, 20 % mild,
  20 % strict), night (sunset − Δ to sunrise, pvlib), season, hub wind and hub temperature.

## Notes

- **No precipitation criterion** in the bat masks: the ERA5 table has no precipitation (conservative, slightly
  higher losses than permits with a rain exception).
- Grid area targets A4 (NO), A5 (Mitte/W) and A6 (Süd) are **assumptions** under the constraint of the national
  sum; 2026 targets = 2025. A6 has no grid layer.
- Prices: day-ahead DE-LU, hourly until 2025-09-30 (piecewise constant on the quarter-hours), 15 min from
  2025-10-01; CC BY 4.0 Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de via energy-charts.info. Wind onshore forecast/actual and
  installed capacity: energy-charts.info (Fraunhofer ISE).
- The p0 status and the participation of a node are not identifiable from public data (report 6.9); the
  realised shares of our parks are reported against the expected client shares, not re-drawn.
