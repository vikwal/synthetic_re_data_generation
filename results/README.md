# results/

Quantitative results and figures underlying the round-2 revision of the paper
("A Measurement- and ERA5-Driven Simulation Framework for Synthetic Wind
Power Time Series Accounting for Turbine Aging"). Generated by
`scripts/round2/make_summary.py` and `scripts/round2/regen_paper_figs.py`
from the full run in `round2/` + `scripts/round2/`.

## `quantitative/`

- `summary.md` — the two headline tables cited throughout Section 5: the
  per-configuration ladder medians (M0-M4, S1-S4) and the pairwise
  statistical contrasts isolating each chain component's contribution
  (aging, downscaling, MOST stability, wake).
- `scores.csv`, `ladder_results_flat.csv` — full per-farm, per-configuration
  metrics behind the ladder table (Table `tab:ladder`) and Appendix A/B means.
- `error_decomposition*.csv` — Murphy skill-score decomposition (Table
  `tab:murphy`).
- `sobol_indices.csv`, `morris_ranking.csv`, `main_effects.csv` — sensitivity
  analysis (Section 5.8).
- `wilcoxon_key_comparisons.csv`, `contrasts.csv`, `rung_overview.csv` —
  aging/degradation contrasts (Table `tab:aging_rungs`).
- `stability_stratified.csv`, `blh_flags.csv` — MOST stability stratification
  (Table `tab:pl_stability`, `tab:pl_height`).
- `rf_classifier_heldout.csv`, `outlier_shares*.csv`, `wp4_*.csv` — terrain
  classifier held-out performance, DBSCAN outlier shares, aging curve/fleet
  efficiency checks referenced in Section 5.

Note: some of these CSVs include intermediate configuration variants (e.g.
gate/QM ablations) beyond the M0-M4/S1-S4 configurations reported in the
paper text — these are the full sensitivity/ablation sweep, kept for
transparency and reproducibility.

## `figures/`

The exact figures embedded in the manuscript (`pictures_revision/fig*.png`
in the paper repo), named after their `\label` in the paper:
`fig_class_map`, `fig_acf`, `fig_bars_r2`, `fig_ladder_dumbbells`,
`fig_placebo_hist`, `fig_morris`, `fig_main_effects`, `fig_error_distribution`,
plus `fig_bias_vs_age.png` and `sobol_indices.png` (supporting figures
discussed in the text but not all individually embedded).

`figures/power_curves/` contains the operational power-curve comparison
(framework vs. Renewables.ninja) for **all 13 validation farms**; only the
Saxony-Anhalt plot is embedded in the paper (`fig_pc_saxony_anhalt.png`), the
manuscript explicitly refers readers to this repository for the other
twelve (Section 5, farm-level validation).

Purely conceptual figures not produced by this codebase (idealized power
curve, MaStR age-distribution histogram) live only in the paper's own
`pictures_revision/` and are not duplicated here.

The three 10 m wind-speed comparison figures (`fig3`/`fig4`/`fig5.png` in the
paper, ERA5 vs. Renewables.ninja vs. DWD) are deliberately **not** included
here: they were last generated on the 160-station predecessor of this
dataset and have not yet been refreshed for the current 200-station release
(see `HANDOFF.md`, "Ask 36", paused). Regenerate them from the 200-station
dataset before treating this results folder as fully synchronized with the
manuscript.
